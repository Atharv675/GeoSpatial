## Professional (LinkedIn)
Excited to share GeoSentinel — an open-source geospatial intelligence and OSINT fusion platform designed for regional situational awareness. By fusing Copernicus Sentinel-1 SAR and Sentinel-2 optical imagery with real-time ground truth (FIRMS thermal hotspots, ACLED incident data, and GDELT news streams), GeoSentinel delivers deterministic, explainable anomaly detection with zero black-box ML hallucinations. Built with FastAPI, PostgreSQL/PostGIS, React 18, and Leaflet.
